Mobile Security

Mobile Patrol

Visible, professional security patrols at random or pre-arranged times a powerful deterrent to would-be intruders across your property.

Request a Free Quote 0330 229 5997

Guardwise can provide patrols of your property at random or pre-arranged times, acting as a visible deterrent to any would-be troublemakers. Such patrols can be site-specific, or we can provide a local security presence covering a number of properties and streets.

All of our patrols are verified by our Smart Task proof-of-presence system, allowing customers to view and scrutinise patrols through a web-based portal in real time. With NFC tags placed at agreed inspection points, our officers record their presence and movements around a site using their smartphone devices.

This technology ensures complete transparency you always know exactly when your property was checked and by whom, with full written reports provided after every patrol.
