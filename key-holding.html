Key Holding & Alarm Response

We offer a cost-effective key holding and alarm response solution to relieve the pressure of organising key holders within your business. Our SIA licensed guards will rapidly respond to any alarm call, ensure the safety of your premises, and immediately alert police if there are any signs of criminal activity.

As soon as you hand your keys to our security guards, you can guarantee they will respond to any alarm call, attend to any emergency and complete external checks to safeguard against criminal damage whilst ensuring the overall safety of your building and staff.

Our security guards will liaise with the emergency services on your behalf, and can carry out emergency boarding and repairs to completely secure your building from future intruders. For added confirmation, we provide a detailed report of every alarm response we attend.
