K9 Security

Security Patrol Dogs

One security dog can do the work of five guards our NASDU trained handlers and K9 units provide an unmatched security presence and early warning system.

Request a Free Quote 0330 229 5997

It is a well-known fact within the security industry that a security dog can do the work of up to five guards. With their experienced nose and acute senses, a security K9 is both an excellent deterrent and a sensitive early warning system.

Our handlers are all trained to NASDU, NIPDT or BIPDT standards, and all carry out regular continuation training to maintain the highest levels of performance and professionalism on every deployment.

We operate fully within the bounds of the Guard Dogs Act all dogs remain under full control of their SIA Licensed handlers at all times, ensuring complete safety and professionalism on your site.
