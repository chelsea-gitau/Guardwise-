Professional Static Guarding

Security personnel are often the first point of contact for visitors to your site. With this in mind, we specially select and provide only officers of the highest standards, screened to exacting British Standards and fully SIA licensed.

Officers can be uniformed or in plain clothes, and can be utilised as commercial officers to protect staff, stock and premises — or front of house to assist customers and visitors.

Under the Transfer of Undertakings (TUPE) Law, we are able to transfer your existing officers to Guardwise whilst maintaining their existing pay and benefits — making switching to us completely seamless and hassle-free.
